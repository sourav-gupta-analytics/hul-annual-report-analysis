# Executive case study: HUL Financial Performance Intelligence

**Scope:** FY25 restated vs FY26, standalone continuing operations, INR crore. Independent, AI-assisted educational project. Native Power BI dashboard remains pending.

## Decision question
Is HUL's turnover growth translating into sustainable operating profitability, and which segments contribute most to growth?

## Results
| Metric | FY25 restated | FY26 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Turnover | 58,965 | 61,315 | +3.99% |
| Operating revenue | 59,676 | 61,975 | +3.85% |
| Operating costs | 45,532 | 47,511 | +4.35% |
| EBITDA | 14,144 | 14,464 | +2.26% |
| EBIT | 13,022 | 13,278 | +1.97% |
| Pre-exceptional PBT | 13,849 | 13,874 | +0.18% |
| Continuing PAT | 10,653 | 11,020 | +3.45% |
| EBITDA / operating revenue | 23.70% | 23.34% | -0.36 pp |
| EBIT / operating revenue | 21.82% | 21.42% | -0.40 pp |

Turnover change = INR 2,350 crore. Home Care contributed 717 (30.51%), Beauty & Wellbeing 639 (27.19%), Foods 609 (25.91%), Personal Care 392 (16.68%), Others -7 (-0.30%). Beauty & Wellbeing had the fastest segment turnover growth (4.98%).

## Recommendations
1. Investigate cost drivers: operating cost ratio increased 0.36 pp. Prioritize unit-cost, mix and distribution diagnostics; reported aggregate costs cannot prove the drivers.
2. Evaluate Beauty & Wellbeing growth opportunities alongside segment margins, incremental return and competitive position; turnover alone is not an allocation criterion.
3. Protect Home Care performance, the largest turnover contributor. Track volume, pricing and channel mix separately if data is available.
4. Separate tax and exceptional effects from operating performance. Continuing PAT increased 367: EBIT +256, net other income -231, exceptional credit -258, tax expense reduction +600. The tax reduction accounts for a large part of the increase.

## Quality and comparability
FY25 was restated to exclude the Ice Cream undertaking in FY26 reporting following the demerger effective 1 December 2025. Historical FY24/FY25 originally reported values are retained separately; no direct three-year growth series is claimed. Costs are positive expense magnitudes; exceptions are signed credits. Margin denominator is operating revenue, not turnover. Nine arithmetic checks per year reconcile source subtotals and segment totals (18 checks total, zero differences). This is not an independent financial audit. Two-year aggregate data does not support causal or forecasting claims.
